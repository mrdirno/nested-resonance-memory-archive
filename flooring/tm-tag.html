Extra Work Tag

Ground it, skimmed it, filled the joints, pulled cutback, undercut fourteen jambs, moved a fridge nobody moved, or stood in the corridor because the room wasn't clear? Write the tag the day it happened — who told you, what you were told to do, why it's outside your number, and the part everybody argues about in April: what is NOT in this tag. Sixty days from now it's covered up and nobody can see what you were kneeling on.

What you send

One extra, one day, one tag. No prices, no totals, no unit rates and no signature line — a pasted block can't be signed, and the reply with its timestamp is the paper trail. The tag carries no number: the GC's app and the dealer's system own CO and PCO numbering, and a made-up number is how a ticket gets thrown out. If it's 9x9s, black mastic or anything pre-1978, the only line that goes on here is that you stopped and told somebody — this page does not say what it is and neither should the tag.
