What It Read

Every reading, by area, with the limit off your own pail printed beside it. The scrap of drywall goes in the dumpster and the slab gets glued — this is the one record that’s still here ninety days later. Copy it for a spreadsheet, so the rep and the super paste it into their sheet instead of squinting at a photo of a meter face.

Nothing on this page passes or fails a reading. The limit beside each line is the number you read off your own product’s label or the instructions in the box — not a threshold this page set. It sets no moisture limit, no tolerance and no acclimation window, approves no assembly, and is not a manufacturer’s sign-off. It records what you read and what your own label allows, in your words. Once the floor is down the slab can’t be read again — so double-check every number before you send this and before you cover anything.

The job — typed once, rides on every copy
