The Dealer Call

Tomorrow’s goods, called in off a list instead of off your memory. Everything that has to come off the same run — the field material, the fills, the transitions, the stair parts — is gathered at the bottom of the message where the dealer can’t skim past it, together with the attic stock nobody calls in until close-out, by which time the run is gone.

What you send

Every carton, roll, pail, bag and colour on here is what you picked. This page specs nothing and knows nothing about your job — no product, no colour, no coverage, no square feet per carton, no waste factor, and it has never converted a square foot into anything. It holds no lot numbers — the run is whatever you read off the carton in front of you, carried through untouched. It does no takeoff and it never says whether the material or the substrate is right. The counts are the numbers you decided; what a carton yields lives on your own submittal. Double-check it against your spec before you order — and count what comes off the truck before the driver leaves. It’s a field request — not a purchase order, and there’s no money on it.

✦ Built off three flooring hands’ own morning calls. A line missing, or a part your dealer calls something else? Wish for it — it gets added, and you get the credit.
