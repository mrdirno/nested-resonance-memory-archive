Flooring · the writing you put off

The Write-Up Setup

What your number didn’t cover. The prep that ate the morning and becomes the change order. The day report. The shading call somebody says is a defect. The letter for the day they told you to glue it down over your objection — and whatever else you have to write that isn’t on the list. Pick the one you’re stuck with and this hands you the exact instructions to paste into your AI — once. After that it’s: dump the mess, get back something you can send without touching it. It never grades a reading and never calls a floor a defect — it writes down what you measured and what you did.

Nothing you type here leaves your phone — it stays in this browser. What you paste into your AI afterwards is your call, so keep client detail and anyone’s personal information out of it. This builds instructions, not certified data: it never supplies a moisture limit, a flatness tolerance or a pass/fail — double-check anything feeding a warranty or an order against the mill’s own spec.
