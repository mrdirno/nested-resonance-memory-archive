Off The Truck

Two clocks start when the semi backs in. The driver leaves with the paperwork, and a load signed clean makes every dead one your dead one — and a tree in the size you ordered is a season out of the grower, not a week, on a clock that started the morning it was supposed to be here. Count it against your own slip while the trailer is still there, put a row on anything short, wrong, rootbound or dry, and send it the same afternoon. Where you found it is its own field, because a broken leader seen on the trailer and one found in staging on day six are two different conversations with two different people paying.

A count, not a claim — and counts, not dollars. Every row is what you counted against your own packing slip and your own order, both named at the top, and what the tag says is copied off the tag. This page grades nothing to any standard, says nothing about what a plant is worth or why it looks the way it looks, and supplies no size, no spec and no substitute. Physical condition only: rootbound, broken, dry, dumped are things you can see from the tailgate, and what any of it means for the warranty is a different conversation. There are no prices on here; your office owns those.

The job — typed once, rides on every copy

Name both documents — the slip that came with the load and your own order. A shortage counted against a named slip is a fact the yard can work from this afternoon; the same shortage described from memory is a phone call nobody can find in November. And whatever you wrote on the delivery receipt before the driver pulled out, put it in a row here too.

Each line

What the tag says once — it becomes a button and it carries. The delivery and where-you-found-it carry to the next row, because a bad load is never one line. Leave anything blank you didn’t actually count: an empty field is honest and a guessed one is not.

Group by

Tap a row as it moves: blank is counted, then Sent when it went to the yard, then Answered, then Replacement coming, then Landed when it is actually on site. The pencil opens a row to fix a line, add a note, or delete it.

Copy it — the block that goes out this afternoon

Include

The spreadsheet copy is every row with a tab between the columns — it is what your office wants beside the invoice, and it is also your backup: this lives in this browser on this phone, so copy it somewhere the day it matters.
