Sub It Or Wait

The yard doesn’t have it. Not in that size, not this season, not under that name — and the bed it goes in is holding a hundred and forty other plants that are drinking your water while somebody decides. This is the ask, and it stays out of the design: their schedule line rides as an address so you’re both pointing at the same plant, what the yard said rides in the yard’s words, and what you can get rides the way you’d order it. Then the date the answer has to land, and what moves if it doesn’t. Approve one under your own number, or tell me to wait.

What you send

It’s an ask, not a substitution. This page makes no plant call — it doesn’t say a plant is equivalent, suitable or hardy, it supplies no size and no spec, and it reproduces no column of anybody’s schedule; their line number rides here so two people can point at the same plant. Whatever gets approved gets approved on their submittal under their number, not on this note. The plant list stays whoever’s it already is.
