# META OBJECTIVES: NESTED RESONANCE MEMORY

Author: Aldrin Payopay · Reviewed September 6, 2026

## Mission and present evidence

Maintain useful research instruments, make their experiments reproducible, and let controls discipline claims. HALO is the active browser particle laboratory; the [archive lifecycle registry](docs/archive/README.md) identifies the maintained software, experimental research and preserved historical projects. Science, mathematics, physics, art, culture and technology are connected here through runnable work and explicit assumptions, rather than a universal-law assertion.

The original cross-epoch memory protocol **has been registered and run**, over 60 cells at 4,194,304 particles. The [estimator audit](analysis/2026-09-02_cross_epoch_memory_preregistered.md) found a geometric confound: static centrally peaked fields can pass the same Retained-minus-Two-back criterion. This does not establish or retire NRM. Do not repeat the old instruction that registration and data collection have not started. The [frozen protocol](docs/preregistrations/2026-09-02_halo_cross_epoch_memory.md) and its deviations remain the historical record.

## Active work

1. **Make HALO's observations repeatable and inspectable.** The [Observatory bench](docs/halo/OBSERVATORY.md) adds seeded initial conditions, paired magnetic integrators, exact stopping ticks, a sampled energy trace, JSON recipes and interruption tracking. The original modes, presets, fixed tick and GPU physics remain the scientific substrate. The [HALO tests](tests/halo/README.md) report instrument health beside the independent Pages deployment. Require passing instrument evidence before calling a HALO revision validated; a successful site build alone is insufficient.
2. **Repair the memory question before collecting another large grid.** Design a centered, support-matched, monopole-removed estimator; characterize its null rate and injected-effect recovery on the available meshes. State the spatial support and null degeneracy before freezing a new protocol. A positive is meaningful only if the estimator rejects static profiles and resolves the injected effect; a null is meaningful only beside measured power. No positive NRM result is being claimed. The [replacement estimator](docs/halo/2026-09-05_memory_estimator_qualification_protocol.md) was frozen with five falsifiers and scored once on the recorded grid on 2026-09-06: [0 of 60 runs are measurable](analysis/2026-09-06_memory_estimator_qualification.md) (179 of 1,320 run-epochs eligible; result "insufficient support"), because the seeds are near-copies in 17 of 20 conditions, the compressed relic of a collapsed core falls below 8 effective cells in about half of the collapse epochs, and 12 runs are static fields that barely change between epochs. The recorded grid is retired as a qualification set. The next step is a pilot that measures eligible epochs per run and the seed-balance gate on candidate settings, targeting three conditions with three measurable runs each, before any confirmatory grid is registered. That pilot was [frozen on 2026-09-06](docs/halo/2026-09-06_measurability_pilot_preregistration.md) and [run and scored once on 2026-09-07](analysis/2026-09-07_measurability_pilot.md): **0 of 21 runs measurable across seven shipped field configurations**, 37 of 462 run-epochs eligible, and no branch of its own decision rule fires. Its anchors also showed that the recorded 60-run grid was made by a page build replaced two and a half hours after that grid finished, and that the recorded runs come back byte for byte under it. Do not register a confirmatory grid: the successor is a new protocol that predicts relic footprint before separation, records the instrument hash in every run, and renormalises self-gravity when it cuts the drive. Two of those three were worked on 2026-09-09 ([result](analysis/2026-09-09_measurability_noise_and_instrument_identity.md)). **The instrument is now recorded:** a whole-file page hash over-reports (four revisions, two behavioural classes), a digest over the GPU program, named constants, driven presets and `DEFAULTS` reproduces the known partition, and the harness writes an instrument block into every new run without a schema bump, because the frozen scorer gates on the schema string. **Two claims are retired.** "F4 pass" is vacuous in both scored arms: `measurable_both` is 0 in all 11 variants of both, so no variant can register a violation and the robustness gate is blind in exactly this regime — read it as not evaluable. And the pilot's "six independent draws" were three seeds under two instruments; no condition in this programme has two realisations on one instrument, so how much of "measurable" is chance is **not identified** by the recorded data and needs two independent triples at fixed instrument to answer. Note also that the 12-epoch floor is not what binds: six runs clear it and all six are refused by E5b, the seed-balance gate. The drive-cut axis remains untouched and is the successor's remaining job.
3. **Use paired starts for numerical sensitivity.** Compare Euler/exact rotation and substep refinements at matching settings and particle count; repeat across seeds before generalizing. Keep historical presets labeled with the integrator under which they were found. The single-seed A/B bench is an instrument check, not an ensemble result.
4. **Maintain the archive by lifecycle and evidence.** [Component registry](docs/archive/components.json), [inventory audit](tools/archive/audit.py), safe cleanup preview and CI publication gates are the active maintenance tools. Historical work stays discoverable; a runnable entry point and passing check are prerequisites for promoting a dormant project.
5. **Harvest libraries only through the release doctrine.** BCP's optional monitoring and plotting paths have been repaired and now have behavior tests. The latest full-package measurement and remaining release conditions are in the [stewardship report](archive/reports/2026-09-04_archive_stewardship.md). The release workflow enforces greater-than-90% coverage; coverage alone does not establish superiority or justify a release. No library release is authorized by a healthy browser demo alone.

## Deferred until their prerequisites exist

- New memory claims, papers and universality claims await a valid estimator and appropriate external validation.
- Choreography principles and the fifty-page magnetic-family audit need their own source and result review; their presence in the archive is not a fresh validation.
- FPGA synthesis belongs on the Ubuntu build machine; fabrication artifacts remain in private development storage.
- A WebGPU rewrite or another gallery of variants needs a measured performance or scientific benefit before displacing the tested engine.
