# META OBJECTIVES: NESTED RESONANCE MEMORY

Author: Aldrin Payopay · Reviewed September 6, 2026

## Mission and present evidence

Maintain useful research instruments, make their experiments reproducible, and let controls discipline claims. HALO is the active browser particle laboratory; the [archive lifecycle registry](docs/archive/README.md) identifies the maintained software, experimental research and preserved historical projects. Science, mathematics, physics, art, culture and technology are connected here through runnable work and explicit assumptions, rather than a universal-law assertion.

The original cross-epoch memory protocol **has been registered and run**, over 60 cells at 4,194,304 particles. The [estimator audit](analysis/2026-09-02_cross_epoch_memory_preregistered.md) found a geometric confound: static centrally peaked fields can pass the same Retained-minus-Two-back criterion. This does not establish or retire NRM. Do not repeat the old instruction that registration and data collection have not started. The [frozen protocol](docs/preregistrations/2026-09-02_halo_cross_epoch_memory.md) and its deviations remain the historical record.

## Active work

1. **Make HALO's observations repeatable and inspectable.** The [Observatory bench](docs/halo/OBSERVATORY.md) adds seeded initial conditions, paired magnetic integrators, exact stopping ticks, a sampled energy trace, JSON recipes and interruption tracking. The original modes, presets, fixed tick and GPU physics remain the scientific substrate. The [HALO tests](tests/halo/README.md) report instrument health beside the independent Pages deployment. Require passing instrument evidence before calling a HALO revision validated; a successful site build alone is insufficient.
2. **Repair the memory question before collecting another large grid.** Design a centered, support-matched, monopole-removed estimator; characterize its null rate and injected-effect recovery on the available meshes. State the spatial support and null degeneracy before freezing a new protocol. A positive is meaningful only if the estimator rejects static profiles and resolves the injected effect; a null is meaningful only beside measured power. No positive NRM result is being claimed. The [replacement estimator](docs/halo/2026-09-05_memory_estimator_qualification_protocol.md) was frozen with five falsifiers and scored once on the recorded grid on 2026-09-06: [0 of 60 runs are measurable](analysis/2026-09-06_memory_estimator_qualification.md) (179 of 1,320 run-epochs eligible; result "insufficient support"), because the seeds are near-copies in 17 of 20 conditions, the compressed relic of a collapsed core falls below 8 effective cells in about half of the collapse epochs, and 12 runs are static fields that barely change between epochs. The recorded grid is retired as a qualification set. The next step is a pilot that measures eligible epochs per run and the seed-balance gate on candidate settings, targeting three conditions with three measurable runs each, before any confirmatory grid is registered. That pilot was [frozen on 2026-09-06](docs/halo/2026-09-06_measurability_pilot_preregistration.md) and [run and scored once on 2026-09-07](analysis/2026-09-07_measurability_pilot.md): **0 of 21 runs measurable across seven shipped field configurations**, 37 of 462 run-epochs eligible, and no branch of its own decision rule fires. Its anchors also showed that the recorded 60-run grid was made by a page build replaced two and a half hours after that grid finished, and that the recorded runs come back byte for byte under it. Do not register a confirmatory grid: the successor is a new protocol that predicts relic footprint before separation, records the instrument hash in every run, and renormalises self-gravity when it cuts the drive. Two of those three were worked on 2026-09-09 ([result](analysis/2026-09-09_measurability_noise_and_instrument_identity.md)). **The instrument is now recorded:** a whole-file page hash over-reports (four revisions, two behavioural classes), a digest over the GPU program, named constants, driven presets and `DEFAULTS` reproduces the known partition, and the harness writes an instrument block into every new run without a schema bump, because the frozen scorer gates on the schema string. **Two claims are retired.** "F4 pass" is vacuous in both scored arms: `measurable_both` is 0 in all 11 variants of both, so no variant can register a violation and the robustness gate is blind in exactly this regime — read it as not evaluable. And the pilot's "six independent draws" were three seeds under two instruments; no condition in this programme has two realisations on one instrument, so how much of "measurable" is chance is **not identified** by the recorded data and needs two independent triples at fixed instrument to answer. Note also that the 12-epoch floor is not what binds: six runs clear it and all six are refused by E5b, the seed-balance gate. The drive-cut axis was measured on 2026-09-17 ([ring 21](analysis/2026-09-17_drivecut_similarity_measurability.md), scoring the open round's committed runs): realised as a similarity rescaling of the whole force (self-gravity, drive amplitude and magnetism scaled by a common factor), cutting the drive breaks the seed lock — 2 of 5 conditions become measurable (up from 0) and pooling two scales makes the injected-effect recovery falsifier F3 evaluable and pass for the first time — but every arm is a static field (full-field epoch-to-epoch correlation 1.0000), so the measurability is the residual of a collapsed core, F4 and F5 still fail, and the verdict stays "insufficient support." The successor is therefore not another grid under the frozen protocol but a protocol that gates staticness before it scores; nothing about nested resonance memory is decided. [Ring 22](analysis/2026-09-17_corpus_staticness_vs_eligibility.md) (2026-09-17) then asked the prior question of the whole 81-run committed record and, re-verified by a six-agent workflow, found that **0 of 81 runs is both measurable and non-static and no non-static run reaches the 12-epoch floor**: on the grid every one of the six floor-clearers is static (A3 0.99995–0.99999), and the nearest a moving field comes is 10 of 12 eligible epochs (pilot spinchladni sg0.3 gl0, mean A3 0.19) and 8 of 12 (grid), both un-measurable. A staticness gate therefore cannot be validated by re-screening the record — applied to the grid it empties the eligible set — so the successor's condition must be **generated, not selected**, and the one non-static regime within reach is spinchladni sg0.3 gl0. Idea dc635e28 (the frozen-protocol pilot, three rounds) is complete; the next experiment generates at spinchladni sg0.3 gl0 under a staticness-gated protocol, on one pinned instrument with at least two independent triples. That programme has since run (idea bd3fb03c, now archived), on one pinned instrument (`sim_digest 40bfdb68`): [ring 24](analysis/2026-09-18_spinchladni_generation_eligibility.md) generated spinchladni sg0.3 gl0 and found it capped at 10 of 12 eligible epochs by a cross-seed seed lock (E3); [ring 25](analysis/2026-09-18_drivecut_breaks_seedlock.md) broke that lock by halving the drive (fieldExp 2→1.7) — the first non-static measurable condition in the record (19 eligible) — but own-relic detection did not reproduce across seed sets; [ring 26](analysis/2026-09-18_cutdrive_sgsweep_knifeedge.md) swept self-gravity at the cut drive and found sg0.3 a lone measurable knife-edge (1 of 5 conditions). ## Deferred until their prerequisites exist

- New memory claims, papers and universality claims await a valid estimator and appropriate external validation.
- Choreography principles and the fifty-page magnetic-family audit need their own source and result review; their presence in the archive is not a fresh validation.
- FPGA synthesis belongs on the Ubuntu build machine; fabrication artifacts remain in private development storage.
- A WebGPU rewrite or another gallery of variants needs a measured performance or scientific benefit before displacing the tested engine.
