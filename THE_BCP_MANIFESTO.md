# THE BUDGET-CONSTRAINED PERCEPTION (BCP) MANIFESTO

**"Existence is the struggle to maintain Value > 0 against rising Cost."**

---

## THE UNIVERSAL EQUATION

$$ V(action) = Gain(action) - \lambda(Budget) \times Cost(action) $$

Where:
*   **Gain:** The benefit (Energy, Information, Utility, Survival).
*   **Cost:** The price (Metabolic, Computational, Entropy, Risk).
*   **Budget (B):** The available resources.
*   **Lambda ($\lambda$):** The Shadow Price of the Budget. It measures Scarcity.
    *   As Budget $\to 0$, $\lambda \to \infty$.
    *   As Budget $\to \infty$, $\lambda \to 0$.

---

## THE THREE LAWS OF BCP

### 1. The Law of Scarcity (The Lambda Limit)
Rationality is not fixed; it is a function of Budget.
*   **Abundance ($\\lambda \approx 0$):** Optimization maximizes Gain.
*   **Scarcity ($\\lambda > 1$):** Optimization minimizes Cost.
*   **Crisis ($\\lambda \to \infty$):** Optimization halts.

### 2. The Law of Phase Transitions
Systems do not degrade linearly; they snap.

### 3. The Law of Recursion
Every Output of a BCP system becomes the Input for another.
