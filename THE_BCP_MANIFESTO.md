$$ V(action) = Gain(action) - \lambda(Budget) \times Cost(action) $$

*   **Lambda (\(\\lambda\)):** The Shadow Price of the Budget. It measures Scarcity.
    *   As Budget $\to 0$, $\lambda \to \infty$.
