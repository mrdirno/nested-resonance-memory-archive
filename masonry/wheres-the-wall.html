Where The Wall’s At

Wall by wall at quitting time: the course it got to, what’s holding it, and what nobody touches. One message before you’re off the stage.

Nothing on this page braces a wall, rates a wall, or says a wall is safe to load, to backfill against or to work under. A wall not named here is a wall I have said nothing about.

The job — typed once, rides on every message

Three fields and no fourth. No “sent to”, because this one message goes to everybody at once, and no date on the wall — the course is the answer, and a date is what the other man already has and cannot use.

Where each wall got to

Set the wall once — it carries, and it becomes a button. Then it’s pick where it got to, pick what’s holding it, say the course, Add. Tap a row to move it one rung up the ladder — that is the whole end-of-day job: tap each wall up to where it actually got.
