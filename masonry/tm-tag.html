Extra Work Tag

Told to cut in an opening nobody drew, break out a lift and re-lay it, or stand by while another trade got out of your wall? Write the tag before you lay past it — who told you, what you were told to do, why it's outside your contract, and the part everybody argues about later: what is NOT in this tag. Once that wall caps and grouts, nobody can see what you were looking at.

What you send

One extra, one day, one tag. It reads in the same order as the yellow copy on purpose, so you can thumb the triplicate straight off it instead of writing the job twice. No prices, no totals, and no signature line — a pasted block can't be signed, and the reply with its timestamp is the paper trail. The tag carries no number: the book and the GC's app own the numbering.

✦ Something on here in the wrong order, or missing the line your office always asks for? Wish for it — it gets fixed, and you get the credit.
