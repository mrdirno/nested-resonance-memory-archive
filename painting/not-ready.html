Not Ready

Ten trades on this rack write before paint into their own schedules — this is the answer back. Walk the rooms before the crew sets up and name what stops paint, in your words: the mud that’s still soft, the trade that’s still in it, the gouges that were there before you opened a can. Then send the only ask that matters — fix it and tell me when, or tell me in writing to coat it as it sits. Because the first coat makes every one of these conditions yours, forever, and this note is the record it wasn’t.

What you send

It’s a condition list, not a verdict. Nothing on it says anybody’s work failed anything — it says what you found, where, in your words, with your own readings beside limits off your own product data. It sets no moisture number, no temperature, no finish level and no “ready” bar, and it doesn’t stop the job — it hands whoever runs the job the choice, on the record: clear it, or direct it coated as-is.

✦ A hold your mornings hit that isn’t on the list? Wish for it — it gets added, and you get the credit.
