Color Lock

The most re-litigated sentence in this trade is a color picked standing in a hallway — acted on the same afternoon, on no schedule anywhere, and remembered three ways by three people the day the owner walks it. This writes it down while everyone still agrees it was said: who said it, the exact name, number, base and sheen as they gave it, what schedule line it replaces, and how much of the old one is already on the wall. One reply — CONFIRMED — and you tint. No reply, and the crew keeps working to the approved schedule.

What you send

It answers the schedule — it never rewrites it. The color rides exactly as the person stated it and the schedule line it displaces is quoted from the approved schedule; this page never says a color matches anything, reads like anything, or equals anything across brands, and it carries no formula — the counter owns the formula, the schedule owns the intent. If any of the four words — name, number, base, sheen — wasn’t given, it prints as missing instead of guessed.

✦ A line your paint desk always asks for that isn’t on here? Wish for it — it gets added, and you get the credit.
