The Store Call

The 6:30 text to the paint desk — the paint off your schedule, the sundries off a list instead of off your memory, pulled and at will-call before the truck gets there. Everything the shaker touches is gathered at the bottom of the message where the desk can’t skim past it, because a short count on tape is a trip back, and a wrong tint is gallons that don’t go back on the shelf.

What you send

Every product, base, sheen, color and count on here is what you picked. This page specs nothing and knows nothing about your job — no product system, no color, no formula, no coverage rate, no gallons-per-anything, and it never did your takeoff. The gallons are the number you decided; square-feet-per-gallon lives on your own data sheet. Double-check it against your schedule and your submittal before you order — and count what comes off the counter before the van leaves. It’s a field request — not a purchase order, and there’s no money on it.

✦ Built off three paint hands’ own morning calls. A line missing that came in wrong, or a sundry your store calls something else? Wish for it — it gets added, and you get the credit.
