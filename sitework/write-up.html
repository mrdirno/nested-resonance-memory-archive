The Write-Up Setup

What was in the ditch before you put it back — because nobody is ever opening it again to look. The rock nobody drew. The morning a line got hit and every minute on the clock mattered. What you hauled and how you counted it. The grade you left and what it was shot to — and whatever else you have to write that isn’t on the list. Pick the one you’re stuck with and this hands you the exact instructions to paste into your AI — once. After that it’s: dump the mess in the truck, get back something you can send without touching it. It never sets a compaction number, never says a locate was good, and never tells anybody why it settled — it writes down what you measured and what you did.

Nothing you type here leaves your phone — it stays in this browser. What you paste into your AI afterwards is your call, so keep customer detail and anyone’s personal information out of it. This builds instructions, not certified data: it never supplies a trench depth, slope or soil class, a compaction figure, a bedding class, a cover depth or a test pressure, and it never states that a locate was current or a line was clear. Check anything feeding a dig, a pay application or a safety decision against your own plan, spec and geotech report, the utility owner, and your competent person on site.
