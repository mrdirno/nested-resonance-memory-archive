The message that goes next to the link. Which file to open, what each thing is for, and the couple of things that aren’t in there — written so they can forward it to their boss without editing it.

What you send

There is no clock on this page, and there never will be. Every delivery template going carries some version of “considered approved if I don’t hear back” — that is a term of your agreement, not something a delivery note can create, and a client who spots one reads the whole message as fine print. This page also says nothing about what anyone is allowed to do with the file, nothing about formats or platforms, and nothing about money. Everything you type stays in this browser — it saves on this device so the next drop is a restore and two taps, and it goes nowhere else.

✦ A line here that isn’t how you’d say it, or the one thing you always forget to mention when you send files? Wish it better — it gets built, and you get the credit.
