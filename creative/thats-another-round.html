That’s Another Round

“Just one small thing” landed after the rounds you agreed. Write it the day they ask, while it’s still one thing and still cheap — who asked, what they actually asked for, why it’s outside, and where that leaves the delivery date. Then send it and get on with the cut.

What you send

It’s a heads-up, not a bill. There is no price on this, no number, no fee and no signature line — whatever you use to invoice owns all of that. This page only ever repeats back the deal you typed in; it has no opinion about how many rounds is normal, because there is no such number. Send it the same day, keep it warm, and end it with the choice: in and the date moves, or after delivery and it doesn’t.
