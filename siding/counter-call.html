What you send

Every square, box, piece, colour and count on here is what you typed. This page specs nothing and knows nothing about your wall — no squares figured off a drawing, no fastener, no gap, no exposure, no clearance, no gutter or downspout size, no hanger layout, no flashing detail, no rating and no warranty term. All of that is on the printed instructions you were handed, your selection and your contract. Double-check it before you send it — it’s not a substitute for the manufacturer’s instructions or the code — and count what comes off the truck before the driver leaves. It’s a field request, not a purchase order.
