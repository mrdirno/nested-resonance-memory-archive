Extra Work Tag

Found it after tear-off, or told to do something that isn't yours? Write the tag on the open deck — who told you, what you were told to do, why it's outside your contract, and the part everybody argues about later: what is NOT in this tag.

What you send

One extra, one day, one tag. It reads in the same order as the yellow copy on purpose, so you can thumb the triplicate straight off it instead of writing the job twice. No prices, no totals, and no signature line — a pasted block can't be signed, and the reply with its timestamp is the paper trail. The tag carries no number: the book and the GC's app own the numbering.
