Paving · on the base, before the plant ships

Not Ready to Pave

Every trade on the site counts down to “before you pave,” and you are the one who finds out at six in the morning which of them didn’t make it. Walk the section before the trucks are ordered and name what stops the paving, in your words: the base that pumps under the water truck, the curb that isn’t cured, the lids that aren’t to grade, the trench somebody’s still in, the conex nobody moved. Then send the only ask that matters — fix it and tell me when, or tell me in writing to pave it as it sits. Because a mat over a base that wasn’t ready is your warranty, and the plant doesn’t take the loads back.

What you send

It’s a condition list, not a verdict. Nothing on it says anybody’s base, curb or trench failed anything — it says what you found, where, in your words, with anything you measured taken off your own string line and set beside what your own sheet says. It states no density, no compaction number, no thickness, no grade tolerance, no temperature and no threshold of any kind, it never says a base is ready or passed — that is the lab’s numbered report and the civil’s call — and it doesn’t stop the job: it hands whoever runs the job the choice, on the record: clear it, or direct it paved as it sits.

✦ A hold your mornings hit that isn’t on the list? Wish for it — it gets added, and you get the credit.
