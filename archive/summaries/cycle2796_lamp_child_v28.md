## Methodology
- **Algorithm:** 8-fold Symmetric Interference Pattern.
- **Math:** Summation of 4 Plane Waves oriented at 45-degree intervals (`0, 45, 90, 135`).
- **Structure:** This creates an aperiodic tiling known as the Ammann-Beenker tiling (the octagonal cousin of Penrose tiling).
