## Methodology
- **Algorithm:** Takagi Curve (Blancmange Function) Extrusion.
- **Math:** `Sum (1/2^n) * triangle_wave(2^n * x)`. A fractal sum of sines/triangles that creates a bumpy, self-similar "pudding" profile.
- **Mapping:** The curve function modulates the Z-height of horizontal bands wrapping around the cylinder.
- **Structure:** 
  - **Fractal Waves:** The primary surface is a series of stacked, undulating ribbons defined by the Takagi function.
  - **Reinforcement:** Vertical ribs and a background lattice connect the floating ribbons into a coherent shell.
