# Cycle 2823: The De Rham Curve (Child 115)

**Date:** Dec 4, 2025
**Objective:** Create "Child 115" lamp design based on favorites, exploring Generalized Fractal Curves via Corner Cutting.

## Methodology
- **Algorithm:** De Rham Curve (Iterated Corner Cutting).
- **Fractal:** A continuous curve generated by iteratively slicing the corners of a polygon with a ratio `w=0.15` (sharper than Chaikin's 0.25).
- **Geometry:** The base polygon is an 8-pointed star, which evolves into a smooth but intricate fractal loop.
- **Mapping:** The curve is wrapped around a cylinder in a spiral, mapping 2D coordinates `(angle, radius)` to 3D `(theta, z)`.
- **Structure:** Voxel Painting with a thick radius (8mm) creates a solid, rope-like structure, reinforced by a background Gyroid lattice.

## Principles Adhered To
- **Flow:** The spiral wrapping creates continuous vertical movement.
- **Breath:** The gaps between the curve segments and the lattice provide porosity.
- **Jaw-Dropping:** A fractal knot that looks like a smooth, alien vine.
