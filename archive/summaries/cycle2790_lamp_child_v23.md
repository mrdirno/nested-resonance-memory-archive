# Cycle 2790: The Weaire-Phelan 2 (Child 88)

**Date:** Dec 4, 2025
**Objective:** Create "Child 88" lamp design based on favorites, perfecting the Ideal Foam.

## Methodology
- **Algorithm:** A15 Crystallographic Phase (Beta-Tungsten).
- **Physics:** This structure is the solution to the "Kelvin Problem" (minimal surface area partition of space into equal volume cells).
- **Math:** `2 * (Cyclic Sum cos(x)cos(y)) - 0.5 * (Sum cos(2x))`
- **Structure:** Creates a complex arrangement of polyhedral cells (12-sided and 14-sided) that pack perfectly.
- **Lattice:** The "Walls" of the cells form the solid structure.
