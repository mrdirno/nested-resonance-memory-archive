# Cycle 2812: The Cantor Dust (Child 105)

**Date:** Dec 4, 2025
**Objective:** Create "Child 105" lamp design based on favorites, exploring Fractal Dust.

## Methodology
- **Algorithm:** Recursive Cantor Set Extrusion.
- **Fractal:** The Cantor Set is defined by iteratively removing the middle third of a line segment.
- **3D Projection:** This 1D logic is applied to X and Y coordinates to create a forest of square pillars.
- **Dissolution:** The iteration depth increases with height (Z), causing the solid pillars at the base to fracture into finer and finer "dust" at the top.
- **Reinforcement:** A Gyroid lattice and horizontal "floor" plates are unioned with the fractal to ensure the disconnected dust particles remain suspended in a physical structure.
