# Cycle 2815: The Vicsek Fractal (Child 108)

**Date:** Dec 4, 2025
**Objective:** Create "Child 108" lamp design based on favorites, exploring Cross Fractals.

## Methodology
- **Algorithm:** Vicsek Fractal (Box Fractal) Iterated Function System.
- **Geometry:** A square is subdivided into 9 squares, and the 4 corner squares are removed, leaving a cross shape. This is repeated recursively.
- **Mapping:** The pattern is wrapped around a cylinder with a slight twist.
- **Structure:** Reinforced by a thick Gyroid lattice and horizontal ribbing to ensure the diagonal gaps in the fractal do not cause physical separation.
