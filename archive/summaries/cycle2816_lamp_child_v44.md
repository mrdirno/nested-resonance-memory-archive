# Cycle 2816: The Moran Process (Child 109)

**Date:** Dec 4, 2025
**Objective:** Create "Child 109" lamp design based on favorites, exploring Stochastic Growth.

## Methodology
- **Algorithm:** Moran Process (Stochastic Evolutionary Simulation).
- **Process:** 
  1.  **Simulation:** A coarse grid tracks a population of cells. Cells reproduce into empty neighbors with a bias towards upward and inward growth, simulating an organism seeking light or structure.
  2.  **Upscaling:** The active cells are converted into dense 6mm blocks in the fine grid.
  3.  **Texturing:** A Gyroid mask carves out the interior of the blocks, turning them from solid voxels into porous foam.
  4.  **Reinforcement:** A background "Safety Net" lattice ensures that any disconnected clusters of growth are caught and bound to the main structure.
