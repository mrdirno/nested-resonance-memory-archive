# Cycle 2792: The Lissajous Knot 2 (Child 89)

**Date:** Dec 4, 2025
**Objective:** Create "Child 89" lamp design based on favorites, exploring Parametric Knots.

## Methodology
- **Algorithm:** Parametric Distance Field Painting.
- **Curve:** A 3D Lissajous knot defined by harmonic ratios `5:7:9`.
- **Math:** `x=sin(5t), y=sin(7t), z=sin(9t)`.
- **Lattice:** Instead of a solid tube, the volume of the knot is filled with a "Wireframe" Gyroid lattice.
- **Technique:** "Voxel Painting" - iterating 15,000 points along the curve and stamping a local lattice mask into the voxel grid.

- **Jaw-Dropping:** A knot that passes through itself in a lattice form.
