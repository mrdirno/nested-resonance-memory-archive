# Cycle 2821: The Pythagoras Tree (Child 113)

**Date:** Dec 4, 2025
**Objective:** Create "Child 113" lamp design based on favorites, exploring Recursive Branching.

## Methodology
- **Algorithm:** Pythagoras Tree Fractal.
- **Geometry:** Constructed by recursively placing two squares on top of a base square, forming a right triangle gap.
- **3D Projection:** The 2D tree structure is mapped onto a sphere surface `(u, v) -> (theta, phi)` with a twist.
- **Materialization:** Voxel Painting renders the squares as thick plates or blocks on the surface.
- **Structure:** A secondary Gyroid lattice and horizontal ribs ensure the disconnected "leaves" (smallest squares) remain attached to the main body.
