# Cycle 2779: The Void Manifold (Child V13)

**Date:** Dec 4, 2025
**Objective:** Create "Child V13" lamp design based on favorites, exploring Negative Space geometry.

## Methodology
- **Algorithm:** Constructive Solid Geometry (Difference).
- **Base Solid:** Thick-walled Spherical Shell.
- **Subtractive Elements:**
  - **Spiral Voids:** A series of 60 spherical "anti-matter" pockets spiraling up the surface, varying in radius.
  - **Twisted Channels:** 8 vertical cylinders that rotate around the central axis, carving deep flutes into the shell.
- **Inversion:** Unlike lattice generation where we define where matter *is* (Iso-surface), here we define where matter *is not* (Boolean subtraction).
