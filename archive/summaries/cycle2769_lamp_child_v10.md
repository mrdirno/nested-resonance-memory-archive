# Cycle 2769: The Fractal Singularity (Child V10)

**Date:** Dec 4, 2025
**Objective:** Create "Child V10" lamp design, the finale of the Favorites Child series, demonstrating Infinite Recursion.

## Methodology
- **Algorithm:** Fractal Gyroid Summation (FBM).
- **Math:** `Sum( Amplitude[i] * Gyroid( Frequency[i] * p ) )` for `i = 1..3`.
- **Structure:**
  - **Octave 1:** Base Gyroid (Large Voids).
  - **Octave 2:** Surface Detail (Medium structure).
  - **Octave 3:** Texture (Fine grit).
- **Effect:** A sponge-within-a-sponge aesthetic that creates insane surface area and complexity while maintaining structural validity.
