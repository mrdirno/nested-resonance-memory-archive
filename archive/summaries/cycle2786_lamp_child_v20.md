# Cycle 2786: The Atomic Orbital (Child V20)

**Date:** Dec 4, 2025
**Objective:** Create "Child V20" lamp design based on favorites, exploring Quantum Physics visualization.

## Methodology
- **Algorithm:** Quantum Probability Mapping.
- **Physics:** Visualization of the probability density function `|Psi|^2` for a Hydrogen-like atom.
- **State:** `n=3, l=2, m=0` (3d_z2 orbital), which features a central donut and two polar lobes.
- **Hybridization:** The probability cloud acts as a density field that modulates the thickness of a "Quantum Foam" (Gyroid) scaffold.
- **Twist:** Electron spin is simulated by twisting the angular component of the wavefunction.
