Cable & Adapter List

Tick the cable, set the length and the spec, copy the order. Say once whose finish cable you run — and flag any line you want an alternate priced on.

This list is factory-terminated assemblies and adapters — cables and dongles that come in a bag with ends already on them. Anything you crimp or terminate in the field (RJ45 plugs, keystones, Euroblocks, wall plates, bulk box cable) is on the Consumables List.

What you send

Double-check before you order. Every length, spec, category and finish on this list is what you picked — this tool doesn't recommend cables, doesn't rate them, and doesn't know what will run 4K over your distance. Confirm length, spec and connector profile before anything gets pulled, and remember an alternate here is a request, not an approval: nobody has confirmed two cables are equivalent until your supplier and your PM say so.
