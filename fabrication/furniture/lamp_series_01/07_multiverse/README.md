# HELIOS SERIES 01: DESIGN 07 - THE MULTIVERSE

## Concept
"The Multiverse" envisions a foam of independent bubble universes. The design uses a sphere-packing algorithm to create a porous, bubbling structure.

## Artifacts

### 1. The Bubble Foam (Shade)
*   **File:** `multiverse_shade.stl`
*   **Geometry:** A Boolean union of 300 random spheres distributed on a spherical shell.
*   **Features:** 
    *   1-Inch Wall Thickness (Robustness).
    *   **SOLID TOP CAP** (Mounting Fix) ensuring robust connection.
    *   Hand Access Void (Standard).

### 2. The Stack (Shaft)
*   **File:** `multiverse_shaft.stl`
*   **Geometry:** A stack of 6 modulated spheres/bubbles, creating a segmented, organic column.
*   **Features:** 
    *   14mm Central Cable Channel.
    *   10mm End Recesses for Hardware.

### 3. The Aggregate (Base)
*   **File:** `multiverse_base.stl`
*   **Geometry:** A cluster of bubbles forming a stable mound.
*   **Features:**
    *   Wire Channel (Bottom).
    *   Feet Recesses.
    *   Hardware Recess.

## Fabrication
*   **Material:** PETG/PLA (Transparent or White recommended).
*   **Layer Height:** 0.2mm.
*   **Infill:** 15-20%.
*   **Supports:** 
    *   **Shade:** Bubble structures are generally self-supporting if overhangs are < 45 degrees. Some internal support might be needed for the top cap.
    *   **Shaft:** None required (Vertical stack).
    *   **Base:** None required (Flat bottom).
