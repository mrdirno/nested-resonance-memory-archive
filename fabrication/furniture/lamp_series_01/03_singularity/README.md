# HELIOS SERIES 01: DESIGN 03 - THE SINGULARITY

## Concept
"The Singularity" represents the center of a black hole—a point of infinite density and curvature. The design language uses spirals, stretching (spaghettification), and deep gravity wells.

## Artifacts

### 1. The Event Vortex (Shade)
*   **File:** `singularity_shade.stl`
*   **Geometry:** A Gyroid lattice subjected to a radial torsion field (Vortex). The lattice twists 0.05 radians per mm of radius, creating a swirling effect towards the center.
*   **Features:** 
    *   1-Inch Wall Thickness (Robustness).
    *   Spider Fitter V7 (Standard Mounting).
    *   Hand Access Void (Standard).

### 2. The Spaghetification (Shaft)
*   **File:** `singularity_shaft.stl`
*   **Geometry:** A Hyperboloid of One Sheet, textured with vertical ribs that stretch and compress with the surface curvature, mimicking tidal forces.
*   **Features:** 
    *   14mm Central Cable Channel.
    *   10mm End Recesses for Hardware.

### 3. The Gravity Well (Base)
*   **File:** `singularity_base.stl`
*   **Geometry:** A dense, low-profile cylinder with a parabolic depression on the top surface, representing the collapse of space-time.
*   **Features:**
    *   Wire Channel (Bottom).
    *   Feet Recesses.
    *   Hardware Recess.

## Fabrication
*   **Material:** PLA/PETG (Matte Black or Dark Grey recommended).
*   **Layer Height:** 0.2mm.
*   **Infill:** 15-20% (Gyroid).
*   **Supports:** 
    *   **Shade:** None required (Gyroid is self-supporting).
    *   **Shaft:** None required (Vertical print).
    *   **Base:** None required (Flat bottom).
