# HELIOS SERIES 01: DESIGN 09 - THE NEUTRON STAR

## Concept
"The Neutron Star" (Magnetar) visualizes the intense magnetic fields surrounding a super-dense stellar remnant. The design uses twisted toroidal ribs to represent flux lines.

## Artifacts

### 1. The Magnetosphere (Shade)
*   **File:** `neutron_shade.stl`
*   **Geometry:** A shell defined by twisted ribs that follow toroidal magnetic field lines. The ribs spiral 180 degrees from top to bottom.
*   **Features:** 
    *   1-Inch Wall Thickness (Robustness).
    *   **SOLID TOP CAP** (Mounting Fix).
    *   Hand Access Void.

### 2. The Flux Tube (Shaft)
*   **File:** `neutron_shaft.stl`
*   **Geometry:** A cylinder with 12 twisted magnetic flux tubes (ribs) running vertically, representing the collimated energy jets.
*   **Features:** 
    *   14mm Central Cable Channel.
    *   10mm End Recesses for Hardware.

### 3. The Accretion Disk (Base)
*   **File:** `neutron_base.stl`
*   **Geometry:** A swirling, spiralled disk representing the matter falling into the star. The surface is modulated by spiral arms.
*   **Features:**
    *   Wire Channel (Bottom).
    *   Feet Recesses.
    *   Hardware Recess.

## Fabrication
*   **Material:** PETG/PLA (Metallic colors like Silver, Gold, or Copper recommended).
*   **Layer Height:** 0.2mm.
*   **Infill:** 15-20%.
*   **Supports:** 
    *   **Shade:** None required (Twist angles safe).
    *   **Shaft:** None required.
    *   **Base:** None required.
