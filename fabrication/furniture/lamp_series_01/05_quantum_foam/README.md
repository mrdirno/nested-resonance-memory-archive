# HELIOS SERIES 01: DESIGN 05 - THE QUANTUM FOAM

## Concept
"The Quantum Foam" represents the turbulent nature of space-time at the Planck scale. The design uses bubbling, cellular structures to visualize probability clouds and vacuum fluctuations.

## Artifacts

### 1. The Probability Cloud (Shade)
*   **File:** `quantum_shade.stl`
*   **Geometry:** A Schwarz P (Primitive) minimal surface. This divides space into cubic cells resembling a dense foam or bubble lattice.
*   **Features:** 
    *   1-Inch Wall Thickness (Robustness).
    *   Spider Fitter V7 (Standard Mounting).
    *   Hand Access Void (Standard).

### 2. The Wave Function (Shaft)
*   **File:** `quantum_shaft.stl`
*   **Geometry:** A cylinder with a sine-wave envelope modulating its radius, representing a standing probability wave.
*   **Features:** 
    *   14mm Central Cable Channel.
    *   10mm End Recesses for Hardware.

### 3. The Observer (Base)
*   **File:** `quantum_base.stl`
*   **Geometry:** A clean, minimalist cylinder. In quantum mechanics, the observer collapses the wave function; this base represents the stable, measured reality grounding the chaotic foam above.
*   **Features:**
    *   Wire Channel (Bottom).
    *   Feet Recesses.
    *   Hardware Recess.

## Fabrication
*   **Material:** PLA/PETG (Matte White or Light Grey recommended).
*   **Layer Height:** 0.2mm.
*   **Infill:** 15-20%.
*   **Supports:** 
    *   **Shade:** None required (Minimal surface).
    *   **Shaft:** None required (Vertical print).
    *   **Base:** None required (Flat bottom).
