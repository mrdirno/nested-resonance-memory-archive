# HELIOS SERIES 01: DESIGN 06 - THE DARK MATTER

## Concept
"The Dark Matter" visualizes the invisible scaffolding of the universe. The design uses a Voronoi-based "Cosmic Web" structure, where the material exists only in the filaments connecting invisible voids.

## Artifacts

### 1. The Cosmic Web (Shade)
*   **File:** `dark_shade.stl`
*   **Geometry:** A 3D cellular noise structure (Voronoi/Worley Noise edges). It resembles a neural network or the large-scale structure of the universe.
*   **Features:** 
    *   1-Inch Wall Thickness (Robustness).
    *   Spider Fitter V7 (Standard Mounting) with **SOLID TOP RIM** for guaranteed connection.
    *   Hand Access Void (Standard).

### 2. The Filamentary Void (Shaft)
*   **File:** `dark_shaft.stl`
*   **Geometry:** A twisted bundle of 8 strands that rotate 1.5 times around a central (hidden) core, representing the gravitational lensing effects of dark matter.
*   **Features:** 
    *   14mm Central Cable Channel (Hidden inside bundle).
    *   10mm End Recesses for Hardware.

### 3. The Void Anchor (Base)
*   **File:** `dark_base.stl`
*   **Geometry:** A dense cellular lattice, mirroring the shade but with thicker webs for stability. The negative space is emphasized.
*   **Features:**
    *   Wire Channel (Bottom).
    *   Feet Recesses.
    *   Hardware Recess.
    *   Solid Rim and Center for structural integrity.

## Fabrication
*   **Material:** PETG/PLA (Transparent or Translucent Blue/Purple recommended).
*   **Layer Height:** 0.2mm.
*   **Infill:** 100% (The strands are thin enough that they should be solid).
*   **Supports:** 
    *   **Shade:** Minimal supports might be needed for long horizontal bridges, but Voronoi is generally printable.
    *   **Shaft:** None required (Twist angle is safe).
    *   **Base:** None required.
