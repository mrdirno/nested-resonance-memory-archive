# HELIOS SERIES 01: DESIGN 10 - THE FINAL THEORY

## Concept
"The Final Theory" (Geometric Unity) represents the unification of all forces. The design uses high-dimensional projection (Quasicrystal/E8 lattice) to create a structure that appears ordered but never repeats (aperiodic).

## Artifacts

### 1. The Quasicrystal (Shade)
*   **File:** `final_shade.stl`
*   **Geometry:** A 3D projection of a 6D hypercubic lattice (Icosahedral Quasicrystal). Created by summing cosine waves along the 6 axes of an icosahedron.
*   **Features:** 
    *   1-Inch Wall Thickness (Robustness).
    *   **SOLID TOP CAP** (Mounting Fix).
    *   Hand Access Void.

### 2. The E8 Column (Shaft)
*   **File:** `final_shaft.stl`
*   **Geometry:** A cylinder with a 5-fold Penrose symmetry relief pattern, representing the E8 Lie Group projection.
*   **Features:** 
    *   14mm Central Cable Channel.
    *   10mm End Recesses for Hardware.

### 3. The Unity Ring (Base)
*   **File:** `final_base.stl`
*   **Geometry:** A stable cylinder with a 5-fold symmetry relief on the top surface, mirroring the shaft.
*   **Features:**
    *   Wire Channel (Bottom).
    *   Feet Recesses.
    *   Hardware Recess.

## Fabrication
*   **Material:** PETG/PLA (Matte White or Marble recommended).
*   **Layer Height:** 0.2mm.
*   **Infill:** 15-20%.
*   **Supports:** 
    *   **Shade:** None required (Overhangs are generally safe in quasicrystals).
    *   **Shaft:** None required.
    *   **Base:** None required.
