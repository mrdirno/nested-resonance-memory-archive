# HELIOS SERIES 01: DESIGN 08 - THE TIME CRYSTAL

## Concept
"The Time Crystal" represents a structure that repeats in time (breaking time-translation symmetry). Visualized as a periodic rotation along the Z-axis (time axis), creating a twisted, crystalline form.

## Artifacts

### 1. The Temporal Lattice (Shade)
*   **File:** `time_shade.stl`
*   **Geometry:** A faceted octagonal shell that twists 90 degrees from bottom to top. The facets are modulated by a "Time Crystal" texture (periodic geometric noise).
*   **Features:** 
    *   1-Inch Wall Thickness (Robustness).
    *   **SOLID TOP CAP** (Mounting Fix).
    *   Hand Access Void.

### 2. The Vortex Prism (Shaft)
*   **File:** `time_shaft.stl`
*   **Geometry:** A hexagonal prism that twists 180 degrees, representing the flow of time through space.
*   **Features:** 
    *   14mm Central Cable Channel.
    *   10mm End Recesses for Hardware.

### 3. The Anchor (Base)
*   **File:** `time_base.stl`
*   **Geometry:** A stable, faceted octagonal base.
*   **Features:**
    *   Wire Channel (Bottom).
    *   Feet Recesses.
    *   Hardware Recess.

## Fabrication
*   **Material:** PLA/PETG (Translucent or Silk/Shiny filaments recommended to catch the facets).
*   **Layer Height:** 0.2mm.
*   **Infill:** 15-20%.
*   **Supports:** 
    *   **Shade:** None required (Twist angle < 45 degrees).
    *   **Shaft:** None required.
    *   **Base:** None required.
