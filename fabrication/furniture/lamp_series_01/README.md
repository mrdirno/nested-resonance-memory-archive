## Fabrication Standards (The Void Revision)
All designs in this series adhere to the **V4/V7 Quality Assurance** standards:

*   **Shades (V7):** 14mm Hole, Spider Fitter (Hub+Spokes), 4mm Rims, 1-inch Max Wall.
*   **Bases (V4):** 14mm Hole, 8x8mm Wire Channels, 4x Rubber Feet Recesses, Solid Core.
*   **Shafts (V4):** 14mm Internal Clearance, Solid End Caps for mating.

## Hardware Requirements
*   **Threaded Rod:** 1/8 IP (standard lamp nipple) or M10.
*   **Socket:** Standard E26/E27.
*   **Material:** PETG (Shades), PLA/PETG/CF (Bases/Shafts).
