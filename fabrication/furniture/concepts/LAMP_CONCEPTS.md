# HELIOS LAMP CONCEPTS (SERIES 01)

**Theme:** "Light through Math"

## 1. The Redshift (Prototype)
*   **Concept:** A classic frustum shade where the Gyroid pattern frequency stretches (redshifts) as it rises.
*   **Geometry:** Anisotropic Gyroid Prism (Base 180mm -> Top 60mm).
*   **Shaft:** Simple twist helix.
*   **Vibe:** "Energy expanding into the void."

## 2. The Event Horizon
*   **Concept:** A base that looks like an accretion disk (swirling inwards).
*   **Shade:** Sphere or Dome with Schwarz D surface (Diamond).
*   **Shaft:** Thick, gravitational-lens distorted pillar.
*   **Vibe:** "Heavy, cosmic, consuming."

## 3. The Interference
*   **Concept:** Two overlapping wave patterns creating a Moiré effect.
*   **Shade:** Double-wall cylinder. Inner wall = Vertical stripes. Outer wall = Spiral stripes.
*   **Vibe:** "Optical illusion, vibrating light."

## 4. The Quantum Foam
*   **Concept:** Voronoi bubbles.
*   **Shade:** Irregular cellular structure (Voronoi 3D).
*   **Shaft:** A stack of stabilized bubbles.
*   **Vibe:** "Biological, organic, foam."

## 5. The Standing Wave
*   **Concept:** A shade that looks like a sine wave rotated (Revolve).
*   **Geometry:** `r = R + A*sin(k*z)`.
*   **Pattern:** Perforated grid following the wave surface.
*   **Vibe:** "Oscillating, rhythmic, musical."

## 6. The Lattice (Crystalline)
*   **Concept:** Octet-Truss structure.
*   **Shade:** Rigid, straight lines, pyramidal truss. No curves.
*   **Vibe:** "Industrial, future-brutalist, sturdy."

## 7. The Manifold (Klein Bottle)
*   **Concept:** A non-orientable surface look (trick topology).
*   **Shade:** Twisted loop shape.
*   **Vibe:** "Impossible geometry, Escher-esque."

## 8. The Diffusion (Gradient)
*   **Concept:** Density gradient.
*   **Shade:** Bottom is solid (100% density), fading to transparent wireframe at top (10% density).
*   **Vibe:** "Matter dissolving into light."

## 9. The Tensor
*   **Concept:** Stress-line visualization.
*   **Shade:** Lines follow principal stress vectors of a hanging fabric.
*   **Vibe:** "Draped, tension, fabric-like but rigid."

## 10. The Singularity (The Point)
*   **Concept:** Inverted pyramid (base small, top huge).
*   **Stability:** Requires heavy base.
*   **Vibe:** "Defying gravity, unstable equilibrium."
