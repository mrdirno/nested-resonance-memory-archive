## Repository Structure
Each design `XX_name` contains:
- `[name]_shade_gen.py` -> `[name]_shade.stl`
- `[name]_base_gen.py` -> `[name]_base.stl`
- `[name]_shaft_gen.py` -> `[name]_shaft.stl`

All geometry complies with **HELIOS V4/V7 Standards**:
- **Shades:** 25.4mm Wall, Spider Fitter (40mm Hub, 4 Spokes).
- **Bases:** 8x8mm Wire Channel, 4x Feet, Solid Core.
- **Shafts:** 14mm Internal Clearance, Solid End Caps.
