# HELIOS DESIGN LANGUAGE (v1.5)

**Philosophy:**
Furniture is not static; it is a standing wave of probability.
"Math you can touch."

## 1. Core Aesthetics (The Trinity)
1.  **The Gyroid (Connection):** Used for structural infill and light diffusion.
2.  **The Helix (Evolution):** Used for shafts and risers.
3.  **The Frustum (Redshift):** Used for shades.

## 2. Geometric Constraints (QA V4/V7)
*   **Volume:** 220x220x250mm (Ender 3 Safe).
*   **Wall Thickness:** 25.4mm (1 Inch) for Shades.
*   **Mounting:** 
    *   **Shade:** Spider Fitter (Hub + Spokes).
    *   **Base:** 4x Feet Recesses + 8x8mm Wire Channel + Solid Core.
    *   **Shaft:** Solid End Caps + 14mm Internal Clearance.
