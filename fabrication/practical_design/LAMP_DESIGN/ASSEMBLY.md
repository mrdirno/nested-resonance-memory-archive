# Helios Lamp Assembly Guide

**Hardware Bill of Materials (BOM)**

To assemble any Helios Lamp (V4-V8), you will need the following standard hardware:

## Core Components
1.  **Threaded Rod:** 1/8 IP (Standard Lamp Pipe). Length: ~250mm (10 inch) recommended.
    *   *Note:* Shaft height is 200mm. Base height is 25mm. Shade sits on top. 250mm allows room for nuts and socket.
2.  **Hex Nuts:** 1/8 IP (x3).
    *   1x for bottom recess (under base).
    *   1x for top of shaft (securing shaft to base rod).
    *   1x for securing shade to socket (or socket ring).
3.  **Washers:** 1/8 IP (x2) - Optional but recommended for stability.
4.  **Lamp Socket:** Standard E26/E27 (Edison) or E12 (Candelabra) with 1/8 IP cap.
    *   *Recommendation:* E26 Keyless or Pull-Chain.
5.  **Power Cord:** Lamp cord with plug (SPT-1 or SPT-2).
6.  **Feet:** 4x Adhesive Rubber Feet (Diameter < 20mm).

## Assembly Instructions

### Step 1: Base Prep
1.  Insert the **Lamp Cord** through the side tunnel of the **Base**.
2.  Push the cord up through the center hole.
3.  Thread a **Hex Nut** onto the **Threaded Rod** (about 1 inch up).
4.  Insert the Rod into the Base from the top.
5.  Secure from the bottom with another **Hex Nut** (it will sit inside the 30mm Recess).
6.  Tighten. The rod should be standing vertical.

### Step 2: Shaft Installation
1.  Feed the cord through the **Shaft**.
2.  Slide the Shaft over the Threaded Rod.
3.  Ensure the Shaft seats firmly into the **Base Recess** (if applicable) or sits flat on the Base.
4.  Secure the top of the Shaft with a **Hex Nut** (if rod is exposed) or screw the **Socket Cap** directly onto the rod to clamp the shaft down.

### Step 3: Wiring
1.  Tie an Underwriter's Knot in the cord (inside the socket cap) for strain relief.
2.  Connect the wires to the **Socket** (Hot/Smooth to Brass/Gold, Neutral/Ribbed to Silver).
3.  Snap the Socket Shell onto the Cap.

### Step 4: Shade Installation
1.  Place the **Shade** over the socket.
2.  The **Triskelion Spider Fitter** rests on the socket shoulder (or use a harp if preferred, but these shades are designed for UNO/Socket mounting).
3.  Secure with the **Socket Ring** (if threaded socket) or a final finial if using a harp.
    *   *Note:* Helios Shades are designed as "Uno Fitter" style (sit on socket) or "Spider" (sit on harp). The 14mm hole suggests a **Pipe Mount** (finial) or **Socket Nipple** mount. If using a standard E26 socket, you might need a larger hole (40mm).
    *   **Correction:** The generator uses `hole_diam = 14.0` (standard rod). This implies the shade sits on the *rod* above the socket (finial style) or is clamped between nuts on the rod?
    *   *Clarification:* Most table lamps have the shade on a Harp. The Harp sits on the rod.
    *   *Alternative:* The Shade sits *under* the socket? No.
    *   *Uno Fitter:* The hole is usually 33mm-40mm to fit *over* the socket threads.
    *   **Current Design:** 14mm hole. This is for **1/8 IP Rod mounting**.
    *   **Assembly Variant A (Harp):** Use a harp saddle at the base of the socket. Mount shade on harp finial. (Standard).
    *   **Assembly Variant B (Rod Mount):** Run the rod *through* the shade? No, shade is top.
    *   **Conclusion:** These shades are designed for **Harp Finials** (standard 1/4-27 or 1/8 IP).

## Safety
*   Use **LED Bulbs** only (Low Heat).
*   Max 9W LED (60W Equivalent).
*   PLA softens at 60°C. Do not use Incandescent bulbs.
