# SIMULATION LOG 01: Dual-Channel Reactor Efficiency

**Date:** 2025-11-30
**Subject:** Thermal Performance of Artifact 01/04 Pairing
**Tool:** `fabrication/analysis/gyroid_thermal_efficiency.py`

---

## 1. The Objective
Quantify the theoretical performance of a "Resonance Reactor" where **Artifact 04 (The Void)** represents the fluid channels and **Artifact 01 (The Seed)** represents the thermal interface.

## 2. The Results
*   **Geometric Density ($\beta$):** **370 m²/m³**
*   **Comparison:**
    *   **1.2x** Higher Density than Standard Shell & Tube Exchangers.
    *   **0.4x** Density of High-End Micro-Plate Exchangers (at 40mm scale).

## 3. The Physics Insight (Dean Vortices)
While the raw surface area is comparable to standard industry tools, the **Topology** offers a hidden advantage.
*   **Mechanism:** The constant curvature of the Gyroid forces fluids to rotate (Dean Vortices) as they flow.
*   **Effect:** This continuously mixes the fluid, preventing a "stagnant layer" from forming on the walls.
*   **Multiplier:** Heat Transfer Coefficient ($h$) is theoretically **~2x higher** than straight pipes.

## 4. Conclusion
The Artifact 01/04 system is a viable high-performance reactor core. To beat Plate Exchangers, we simply need to **reduce the Unit Cell Size** (Scale Down).
