Came Off The Truck

Two clocks start when the truck backs in. The driver leaves with the paperwork, and a delivery signed clean makes the dent your dent — and a frame or a special leaf is weeks out of the factory, not days, on a clock that started the morning it was supposed to be here. Count it against your own slip while the trailer is still there, put a row on anything short, wrong or beat up, and send it the same afternoon. Where you found it is its own field, because damage photographed on the trailer and damage found on day nine are two different conversations with two different people paying.

A count, not a claim — and counts, not dollars. Every row is what you counted against your own packing slip and your own order, both named at the top. Cartons are identified by opening number and by the supplier’s own line number off the tag — no hardware set contents, no schedule, no keying, ever. Physical condition only: if a tag is gouged, painted over or missing, say so and stop, because what that means belongs to the manufacturer and the authority having jurisdiction, not to the man unloading a trailer at six in the morning. There are no prices on here; your office owns those.

The job — typed once, rides on every copy

Name both documents — the slip that came with the load and your own order. A shortage counted against a named slip is a fact the inside man can work from this afternoon; the same shortage described from memory is a phone call nobody can find in November. And whatever you wrote on the delivery receipt before the driver pulled the gate down, put it in a row here too.

Each line

Mark number once — it becomes a button and it carries. The delivery and where-you-found-it carry to the next row, because a bad load is never one line. Leave anything blank you didn’t actually count: an empty field is honest and a guessed one is not.

The load so far

Group by

Tap a row as it moves: blank is counted, then Sent when it went to the distributor, then Answered, then Replacement coming, then Landed when it is actually on site. The pencil opens a row to fix a line, add a note, or delete it.

Copy it — the block that goes out this afternoon

Include

The spreadsheet copy is every row with a tab between the columns — it is what your office wants for the claim, and it is also your backup: this lives in this browser on this phone, so copy it somewhere the day it matters.
