Doors · at the opening, before the cart comes off the truck

Not Ready To Hang

Five kits on this rack write doors into their own gate ladders — this is the answer back. Walk the openings before the cart comes off the truck and name what stops the hang, in your words: the frame that isn’t grouted, the floor that isn’t in, the wall nobody painted, the electrified opening with nothing pulled to it. Then send the only ask that matters — fix it and tell me when, or tell me in writing to hang it as it stands, where that call is theirs to make. Because once a leaf is on and adjusted, everything wrong behind it reads as your work, and you are the one standing there with a screwdriver explaining it.

What you send

It’s a condition list, not a verdict. Nothing on it says anybody’s work failed anything — it says what you found, where, in your words, with anything you measured taken off your own tape and set beside what your own approved shop drawing says. It states no clearance, no undercut, no tolerance, no closer setting and no label value, it never says an opening is ready, and it doesn’t stop the job — it hands whoever runs the job the choice, on the record: clear it, or direct it hung as it stands. A direction to hang never reaches wire at the frame that could be live or the one you write in yourself — those hold until whoever owns them clears them.

✦ A hold your mornings hit that isn’t on the list? Wish for it — it gets added, and you get the credit.
