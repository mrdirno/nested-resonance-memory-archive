Device Checkout

Log what you put in, as you put it in. Tap a row to move it up — pulled, mounted, terminated, tested, programmed, verified. At the end of the day copy what changed for the huddle, or hand the office the whole list for their spreadsheet. It stays on this phone.

Double-check before you send it. Every tag, type, location and status on this list is what you typed or read off the drawing — this page doesn't space devices, size a battery or a circuit, set a mount height, and it doesn't know what's required or what passed. It's your count of your own rows, not a test report, and the engineer's schedule is still the schedule.

The job

A device count is only true against a drawing revision — put the sheet and the revision in Counted off and nobody has to argue about what it was counted against.

Or paste a column. One tag per line, straight out of whatever you already have.

A browser you haven't opened in a couple of weeks can clear it out, and a new phone definitely will.
