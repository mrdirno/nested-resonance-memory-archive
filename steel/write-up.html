The Write-Up Setup

What the field didn’t match on the stamped set the day you went to set it — the embed cast wrong, the anchor bolts off the column, the beam that came up short. The area you were handed and set anyway because the crane was booked, and who directed you. The day the crane couldn’t fly, with the wind up and a gang standing, and who called it. The walk that came back about the detail and not your work. The RFI you’re still waiting on before you can set. The opening somebody needed framed and nobody drew. The area you handed back and what you told them, in your own words — and whatever else you have to write that isn’t on the list. Pick the one you’re stuck with and this hands you the exact instructions to paste into your AI — once. After that it’s: dump the mess at the gang box, get back something the detailer and the office can forward. It never states a torque, a weld size, a bolt tension, a material grade, a capacity or a cause, and it never says a connection holds or a weld is good — it writes down what you saw, what the sheet said, what you did and who you told.

Nothing you type here leaves your phone — it stays in this browser. What you paste into your AI afterwards is your call, so keep customer detail and anyone’s personal information out of it. This builds instructions, not certified data: it never supplies a torque, a bolt tension, a weld size or pattern, a material grade or a capacity — the shop drawing and the engineer own those, cited as a sheet number; never reproduces a bolt-up log, a weld map, a WPS or a mill cert — that is the special inspector’s record; never says a connection holds, a weld is good, a bolt is tight or the steel is safe to load — the engineer and the inspector own those calls; never says why a field condition happened or who caused it; and it never states that the frame is complete, plumb, bolted up or accepted. Check anything feeding a purchase, a pay application or a safety decision against your own contract, the approved set in your hand, and the people certified to make those calls.
