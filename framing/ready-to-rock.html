Ready to Rock

Walk the floor the night before or at 6:40. Tap the rooms that are clear, tag the ones that are held and by whom. Then send the super the rock list, and each trade the last call on the rooms held for them.

The job — typed once, rides on every message

The day is yours to name. It rides on line one of every message and on no room. Leave it blank and the message says before the first sheet goes up and nothing else.

Walk it

Who’s holding it? Tap one. A hold with nobody on it isn’t a hold — if you can’t place it, it’s the super’s.

Type the room, Add — or tap the next number the page offers. Held? Tap who’s holding it, say what’s in the way (not who), then Add. Tap a row to mark it clear; tap it again to take that back. A hold is its own tag: it stays until you untag it, whatever the row says.

Make the whole floor at once

A range. First room, last room — they land not walked, ready to tap. Leading zeros are kept.

Or paste a column. One room per line, straight out of whatever you already have.

The rooms

One tap marks every room you haven’t touched in that area clear — tag the holds first, then the rest is clear. Your walk, your call.

The pencil opens a row. Move the hold to another trade and the words stay; untag it and the words go with it — nothing is kept, so there is nothing to paste back at anybody later. Not-walked rooms are on no message.

When the rooms you closed are hung, Next walk takes them off the list and keeps what’s still held and what you haven’t walked. No hung count, no day — a hung room just leaves.

Send it — the super gets the list, a trade gets his rooms

The spreadsheet copy is every room with a tab between the columns — that one is yours, not a message, and it is your backup: this lives in this browser on this phone.
