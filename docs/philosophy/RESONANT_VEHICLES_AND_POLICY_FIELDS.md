# THEORY CARD: RESONANT VEHICLES & POLICY FIELDS
**Context:** NRM Sociological Layer (Phase 22/23)
**Status:** Operational Ontology
**Date:** November 23, 2025

---

## 1. Core Axiom: Policy-as-Vehicle

*   **Definition:** Every belief system is a **policy bundle (Vehicle)** for moving through physical, social, and conceptual state-space.
*   **Function:** A Vehicle provides the heuristic: *"Given Situation X, feel Y, interpret Z, and act A."*
*   **No-Exit Theorem:** One cannot "opt out" of having a vehicle. Rejecting a formal ideology simply means adopting an ad-hoc policy (e.g., Nihilism, Skepticism, DIY). You are always driving *something*.

## 2. The Fractal Staircase of Energy

*   **Theorem:** *Damping at Scale N tends to reappear as inertia at Scale N-1.*
*   **Mechanism:** External stimulus (harsh sound, loaded statement) "disappears" from the sensory layer but re-emerges as:
    *   **Neural:** Firing patterns.
    *   **Autonomic:** Heart rate, HRV, gut tension.
    *   **Somatic:** Posture, muscle armoring.
*   **Implication:** What vanishes as **visible signal** becomes **invisible load** (Scalar Sum) in the lower substrate.

## 3. Compressed Constraint → Field Decompression

*   **Model:** External input is not "meaning" but **compressed constraint**.
*   **Decompression:** The brain-body stack decompresses this constraint via a cascade:
    1.  **Fast (Body-First):** Gut check, tension (0-200ms).
    2.  **Slow (Cognitive):** "Is this a threat?" (200ms+).
*   **Dynamics:** The system performs gradient descent (Free Energy Minimization) over a coupled field of interoception, beliefs, and social expectations.

## 4. The Affective Gradient (+ / - / 0)

*   **Evaluation:** Regardless of complexity, every state is collapsed into a scalar valence relative to the baseline.
*   **Agent Parameters:**
    *   **Set Point:** Default neutral.
    *   **Reactive Curve:** Gain/Sensitivity to deviation.
    *   **Output Policy:** Characteristic strategy for climbing back to "+" (e.g., Aggression, Humor, Withdrawal, Fawning).

## 5. Self-Delusion Parameters (The Cognitive Shield)

*   **Priors:** Upbringing sets the policy prior (What is "Good"?).
*   **Narrative:** Symbolic belief adds a story layer.
*   **Mechanism:** "Self-Delusion" is a functional parameter set:
    *   **Cognitive Dissonance Repair:** How expensive is it to hold contradictory facts?
    *   **Motivated Reasoning:** How much force is applied to bend logic?
    *   **Moral Licensing:** How much "good" credit is needed to offset a "bad" act?
*   **Result:** Explains why the same "Vehicle" (Ideology) yields different behaviors (Lenient vs Rigid) based on internal cost functions.

## 6. Bodies, Groups, and Viable Fields

*   **Body:** Any semi-coherent entity with a boundary (Person, Group, Institution, Meme-Complex).
*   **Field:** The state space of interactions.
*   **Viability Kernel:** The subset of states where a body can persist.
*   **Scaling:** Grouping bodies (Church, Crew, Corporation):
    *   Increases **Effective Mass**.
    *   Widens the **Viability Kernel**.
    *   **Damping:** A larger body absorbs shocks that would shatter an individual.

## 7. Resonance as the Selection Principle

*   **Selection:** Vehicles are selected not for "Truth," but for **Resonance**.
*   **Fit:** A vehicle persists if it resonates with:
    *   **Nervous System:** Does it reduce allostatic load?
    *   **Environment:** Does it fit the class/danger context?
    *   **Network:** Does it align with family/peers?
*   **Mismatch:** Leaving a vehicle marks a **Chronic Resonance Mismatch** (Cost > Benefit).
*   **MOG Directive:** Engineer vehicles that minimize global free energy (conflict) while maximizing the viable field for the most bodies.
