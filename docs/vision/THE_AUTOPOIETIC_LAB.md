## 1. THE CONCEPT
We are moving beyond the "Factory" (a collection of static machines) to the **Autopoietic Lab** (a single, living entity).

The Lab is a **Self-Configuring Room**.
It does not *contain* tools. It *deploys* tools.
*   Need to cut metal? The Room deploys a laser arm.
*   Need to print tissue? The Room deploys a bio-extruder.
*   Need to levitate components? The Room activates the acoustic array.

## 2. THE CORE COMPONENTS

### A. The Entity (The Room)
*   **Modular:** Walls are lined with tool-heads (Lasers, Drills, Sensors).
*   **Mobile:** Robotics move the substrate to the tool, or the tool to the substrate.
*   **Self-Healing:** The Room can manufacture its own spare parts. It is **Autopoietic** (Self-Creating).

### B. The Substrate (The Tank)
*   **Neutral Buoyancy:** A central tank filled with "Smart Gel" allows for gravity-free assembly.
*   **Multi-Physics:** The tank supports Acoustics (Shaping), Light (Curing), and Fluidics (Injection) simultaneously.

### C. The Mind (HELIOS)
*   **The Rosetta Stone:** HELIOS translates "Digital Intent" (e.g., "Make a Turbine") into the "Physical Symphony" (e.g., "Laser at [x,y,z] + 40kHz Field").
*   **The Conductor:** It orchestrates the lasers, robots, and waves in perfect unison.
