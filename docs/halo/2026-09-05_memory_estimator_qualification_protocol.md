# Qualifying a replacement estimator for cross-epoch memory in HALO

**Author:** Aldrin Payopay <aldrin.gdf@gmail.com> · **Date:** 2026-09-05 · **License:** GPL-3.0 · **Status:** analysis plan fixed before any score on the recorded meshes was computed. The commit that adds this file, the script, its tests and the synthetic receipt precedes the commit that adds the recorded results; the script refuses to score the recorded grid unless the commit it is told about holds this text and the running script, and the result names that commit and this file's hash.

This qualifies an instrument. It makes no claim about nested resonance memory. The [audit of the registered test](../../analysis/2026-09-02_cross_epoch_memory_preregistered.md) showed that the statistic frozen on 2026-09-02 fires on a static sphere, so it never put the passive-relic claim at risk. The [proposed next step](NEXT_MEMORY_ESTIMATOR.md) asked for a support-matched, re-centred, monopole-removed estimator with predeclared falsifiers, qualified on the recorded meshes before any new particle grid is commissioned. This document fixes that estimator's definitions, its eligibility thresholds and the tests that can fail it.

Two drafts of this plan were reviewed adversarially before the freeze, by independent readers told to refute them, and neither was run on the recorded meshes. The first review found a detection rule with no effect-size floor that fired on anti-correlated relics; an "own relic minus mean stranger" statistic fooled by seed-level relic quality; per-epoch relabelling that ignored epoch autocorrelation; a radial-only falsifier that could not fail; a false-positive check mechanically anti-correlated with the treatment; a recovery threshold that measured baseline signal; and a robustness clause the code did not implement. The second review found that the repaired statistic still read the other two runs' own cells, so one run's effect leaked a quarter of itself into its neighbours; that a switch between two null models was a cliff that re-admitted the quality nuisance when one seed's correlations fell below it; that the recovery falsifier scored template correlation as estimator failure; and that the robustness clause scored variants the plan itself called wrong. Every one of those changed the plan below. The drafts are in the repository's history.

## 1. What a pass means, and what it does not

A pass means: on fields the mesh sees as spherically symmetric the estimator says "not measurable"; on controls where relic identity carries no information by construction it detects at or below the nominal 5 % rate, including controls with seed-dependent noise, seed-dependent drive amplitude, and one seed near the noise floor; a run with no structure of its own is not detected because its neighbour has some; it recovers a labelled passive-relic injection at a stated fraction of the scored block's mass; and its answers hold under reasonable changes of its free choices. It also reports, on the recorded grid, whether a run's angular structure at the relic-map positions follows its own previous relic more than a stranger's, beyond anything explained by a run-quality or relic-quality effect in the run-by-relic correlation matrix.

A pass does **not** establish memory, and no comparison on these meshes can rule seeding out or in. The estimand is the survival, to the closing tick of an epoch, of angular structure at the positions the compressed relic predicts. Any seed-specific structure or parameter whose lag-one self-similarity exceeds its similarity to the strangers' produces it: an angular asymmetry that persists in the lab frame; a seed-specific radial envelope on a shared angular drive; a seed-specific mixture of shared drive modes; a shared pattern that co-rotates or breathes with a seed-specific phase; a wandering centre of mass. So does a relic that seeds the compressed positions and stays there. A relic that seeds the compressed positions and then expands back to the wall within the epoch reads as nothing on the mapped arm and as persistence on the unmapped one. Separating these needs a registered intervention, not a correlation. The confounds are demonstrated on the synthetic controls of §9.

Three limits of a three-seed design are stated here because no rule below removes them. A single run far cleaner than both its strangers cannot be told apart from a single run with memory: when both strangers correlate with nothing, the matrix is a 2 × 2 problem with one degree of freedom. A detection is a statement about the condition's 3 × 3 matrix, not an independent test per run. And the single-run injection of §8 F3 recovers a single-run effect one to one, while a condition-wide effect enters every diagonal cell at once.

The mapped statistic is sensitive only where the radial support of the closing pattern overlaps the compressed relic's, which is the inner half of the cavity. Matter at the wall has no relic prediction. A relic carried by a drive that rotates more than a few tens of degrees per epoch is under-detected or anti-detected, because the estimator has no rotation search; the Spinning Chladni preset rotates. A compressed pattern that drifts by about a cell per epoch is invisible to the block map. Angular structure confined to a thin shell at a radius the compressed relic does not occupy scores zero. A genuine relic whose compressed image is a handful of cells is declared not measurable by E2, which is the intended direction of error.

## 2. Inputs

The 60 recorded runs of the registered grid: two presets (shipped defaults, Spinning Chladni) × self-gravity {0, 0.15, 0.3, 0.5, 0.8} × gain/loss {0, 0.5} × three seeds (777, 12345, 31337), 4,194,304 particles, 24 epochs of 10 s. Each run's 24 density meshes are 32³ little-endian float32, indexed [z][y][x], in units of particles/1024 per cell, from a **nearest-cell deposit**. Each is exported one tick before an epoch boundary and is identical to the density the page keeps as its relic; the rescale itself acts after the first substep of the boundary tick. The three seeds of a preset start from the same particle positions in every self-gravity and gain/loss cell, so the sixty runs are not sixty independent draws. The meshes are local, not distributed by a clone; every file's SHA-256 is written into the result. Nine of the sixty are the [preserved, integrity-audited](../../archive/reports/2026-09-05_halo_memory_input_integrity.md) copies; the script compares them with that manifest and reports "inputs unverified" if any differs. The voided grid is excluded. The JSON epoch rows describe post-boundary state and are not used.

## 3. Geometry, fixed

Cell centres sit at integer coordinates 0…31 on each axis; the cavity centre is 15.5. A shrink by factor *f* about the centre sends a relic cell centre *r* to the current coordinate *x* with *r* = *f·x* − 15.5(*f* − 1). Because the deposit is nearest-cell and the origin is a cell edge, the current cell *x* ∈ {8…23} is the exact image of the relic block {2*x* − 16, 2*x* − 15} on each axis for *f* = 2, and *x* ∈ {12…19} of {4*x* − 48 … 4*x* − 45} for *f* = 4. The **predicted density** at a current cell is the **sum** of the relic cells in its block: the mass a passive relic would deliver to that cell. The lag-one and lag-two arms therefore read every relic cell exactly once, with no interpolation parameter. The shipped `labCorr` read one relic cell per current cell at 2*x* − 16 and 4*x* − 48, half a cell and 1.5 cells off these positions, leaving 7 of 8 relic cells unread on its primary arm and 63 of 64 on its control arm.

Scored blocks: **B2**, the inner 16³ (cells 8…23), for the lag-one arm; **B4**, the inner 8³ (cells 12…19), for the lag-two and region-matched arms.

## 4. Monopole removal, fixed

Cells related by a symmetry of the cube about the centre form an **orbit**: the cells sharing one sorted triple of axis distances (120 orbits on B2, 20 on B4). Each field is replaced by its deviation from its own orbit mean, computed inside the block. This removes exactly any field the grid sees as spherically symmetric, including every nearest-cell deposit of a spherical density and every block sum of one, and also removes any angular pattern that is itself cube-symmetric, which a cube-symmetric drive shares across seeds anyway. Exact radial classes (66 on B2, 15 on B4; cells sharing one squared distance) are a robustness variant: they are exact only for functions of the cell-centre radius and leave a cube-symmetric residual of order 10⁻⁹ to 10⁻⁸ of the block variance on deposited spheres and their block sums. Shell averages of 1.0 and 0.5 cells do not remove a smooth radial function, and "no removal" shows the monopole artefact's size; both are reported contrasts and decide nothing.

## 5. The run-by-relic matrix and the statistic

Scored epochs are *k* = 3 … 24. For a condition's three runs, sorted by seed, and an epoch *k*, the mapped arm forms the 3 × 3 matrix

**M_ij(k)** = Pearson between the residual of run *i*'s current block ρ_k on B2 and the residual of the predicted density from run *j*'s relic ρ'_{k−1}.

The diagonal is a run against its own relic; the off-diagonal is a run against a stranger's. The statistic for run *i* is

**Q_i(k)** = M_ii − P_ii,

where **P_ii** is the value the diagonal cell would take if relic identity carried no information, predicted **from the six off-diagonal entries only**, so that a genuine effect in another run's own cell cannot enter. Two null models are exact for two kinds of nuisance. The **additive** prediction, (M_iB + M_iC + M_Bi + M_Ci − M_BC − M_CB)/2, is exact for M_ij = a_i + b_j. The **multiplicative** prediction is exact for M_ij = a_i b_j, which is what a shared angular drive produces when the seeds differ in noise or in drive amplitude, because a Pearson against a shared template scales as 1/(σ_i τ_j): it is the average of the two exact cross-ratios M_iB M_Ci / M_CB and M_iC M_Bi / M_BC, each weighted by the square of its denominator, clipped to [−1, 1]. The prediction used is *w*·multiplicative + (1 − *w*)·additive with *w* = D²/(D² + 0.05²), where D² is the sum of the two squared denominators M_BC² + M_CB²: a smooth hand-over, not a switch. When the strangers do not correlate with each other there is no shared template for a multiplicative nuisance to act on and the additive form is used; when they do, the cross-ratios are well conditioned. The mean weight over a run's eligible epochs is reported. The first draft used M_ii − mean_{j≠i} M_ij, which is first order in a column effect; the second used an additive prediction that read the other two diagonals, which leaked a quarter of a neighbour's effect and was still second order in a multiplicative one; a third used a hard switch at 0.05 that re-admitted the nuisance below it. None was run on the recorded meshes.

Diagnostics carried beside Q, none of which decides: the uncentred excess M_ii − mean_{j≠i} M_ij, reported beside S so both scales are visible; **D_i(k)** = M_iB − M_iC, the strangers' difference, with a two-sided block sign-flip test whose rejection means the two strangers are not exchangeable for that run; **c_shuf**, the own relic's 32³ cells permuted before coarse-graining (a generator keyed by condition, seed and epoch), the estimator's floor; the raw and residual cross-seed correlation of the current fields; the radial overlap, the Pearson between the orbit means of the current block and of the own prediction, so a zero Q with low overlap is read as "not measurable for seeding" rather than absence; and the orientation diagnostic, the share of the 47 non-identity cube symmetries of the own prediction that score at least M_ii, the maximum over them, the number within 10⁻³ of M_ii (a cube-symmetric drive makes many of them degenerate with the identity), and the same share for a stranger's prediction.

Secondary arms, each a 3 × 3 matrix and contrast of the same form, all reported and none deciding: the **unmapped** arm, current B2 against the relics' own B2 cells at native resolution with no rescale map; the **lag-two** arm on B4 with *f* = 4 and relics at *k* − 2, kept because the registered protocol had it, with its prediction also taken as the inner 2³ of each 4³ block (the trilinear reading) as a reported row; the **region-matched** lag-one arm, current B4 against the *f* = 2 prediction's coarse cells 4…11. Only the lag-one and lag-two arms read the whole relic; the other two read its central 16³.

The unmapped arm is one-directional evidence at best. A mapped contrast that clearly exceeds the unmapped one says the following is position-specific, provided the radial coordinate is conserved within the epoch. The converse shows nothing: persistence of a self-similar envelope, seeding plus re-expansion, and seeding of a scale-free angular pattern all give a mapped contrast no larger than the unmapped one, and the block sum's averaging of eight cells makes the mapped template the cleaner of the two.

## 6. Eligibility, fixed before any score

An epoch is **eligible** for run *i* only if all four hold:

- **E1, support mass:** the mass of ρ_k inside B2 is at least **1 %** of the run's total mass.
- **E2, angular structure:** the participation ratio (Σr²)²/Σr⁴ of the residual, the number of cells that effectively carry it, is at least **8**, and the residual variance is at least 10⁻⁹ of the block variance, for the run's current block **and for all three predicted fields**. A field that is one cell, or that is constant on cube orbits, fails. The registered grid's control arm read 4 or 5 non-zero cells with 99.8 % of the variance in one, a participation ratio near 1; this gate exists to refuse that. The first draft set 8 to 32; the synthetic controls of §9 showed that the coarse-grained relic of a centrally concentrated field with clear angular structure has a participation ratio of 13 to 22, so 32 would have declared measurable fields not measurable.
- **E3, a null that is not a copy:** the Pearson between the residuals of the two **predicted fields** that the matrix actually compares, run *i*'s own and each stranger's, is below **0.9** in absolute value. The first draft gated on the current fields, which are systematically less alike than the coarse-grained templates. An undefined correlation counts as failing.
- **E4:** all nine entries of the arm's matrix are finite.

Two further conditions are judged at the level of the run, over its eligible epochs, because single-epoch correlations are too noisy to judge them; the second review's per-epoch version of the first declared half the Poisson-deposited spheres not measurable through chance flips.

- **E5, no collapse to a 2 × 2:** the strangers' mutual coupling (M_BC + M_CB)/2 and run *i*'s coupling to the strangers (M_iB + M_iC + M_Bi + M_Ci)/4 are averaged over the eligible epochs. If the mutual coupling is consistent with zero (within twice its standard error) while run *i*'s coupling is not and reaches 0.05, the matrix has collapsed to a 2 × 2 in which a seed-quality nuisance and an own-relic effect are indistinguishable, and the run is **not measurable**. Both couplings clearly present (a shared template) and both absent (independent seeds, the ideal case for a passive relic) are fine.
- **E5b, balanced seed quality:** each seed's null value is its own diagonal cell as predicted from the six off-diagonal entries, so a genuine own effect does not enter it, averaged over the eligible epochs. If all three null values are positive, the largest is at least 0.05 and it exceeds **three times** the smallest, one seed sits near the noise floor relative to the others; the rank-one correction then rests on entries it cannot estimate, and the run is **not measurable for unbalanced seed quality**. A seed's mean coupling to the others was tried first and does not work: each coupling entry carries one factor from each seed, so a seven-fold spread of null values shows as a two-fold spread of couplings. The synthetic controls of §9 set this number: at a two-fold spread of seed noise every predictor holds its size; at a four-fold spread the noisiest seed's correlations reach the noise floor and every predictor is biased, the smooth hybrid strongly positive for the strongest seed, the pure cross-ratio mildly positive, a rank-one least-squares fit negative. With every null value below 0.05 there is no shared template and no correction to make, so the gate is off; with null values of mixed sign the off-diagonals are not a shared template at all but seed-specific patterns partly aligned, which the gate does not judge and §1 already names.

A run is **measurable** if it has at least **12** eligible epochs, four full relabelling blocks, and passes E5 and E5b. An ineligible epoch or run is reported as **not measurable**, never as zero memory. The secondary arms inherit the lag-one eligibility plus finiteness of their own matrices.

## 7. Detection rule, fixed

For a measurable run, **S** is the mean of Q_i(k) over its eligible epochs. Under the hypothesis that relic identity carries no information, the relic columns can be cyclically shifted so that every run is paired with a stranger's relic. The permutation test applies a uniformly drawn cyclic shift, **once per block of three consecutive eligible epochs**, recomputing Q on the shifted pairing with the same predictor, draws 4,000 such relabellings from NumPy's default generator seeded 20260905 (a fresh generator per run and arm), and reports *p* = (1 + #{S* ≥ S}) / 4,001. With *n* blocks there are 3ⁿ distinct relabellings, so the smallest attainable *p* is 3⁻ⁿ: 0.012 with four blocks, 0.004 with five; it is reported per run. A run is **detected** if all of: **S ≥ 0.02**, *p* < 0.05, the mean own correlation M_ii over eligible epochs is positive, and it exceeds the mean shuffled-relic floor (waived if the floor is undefined at every eligible epoch). A run with 0 < S < 0.02 and *p* < 0.05 is reported as **below floor**, not detected. Because P_ii reads only off-diagonal entries, S is the own-minus-null excess itself, not a multiple of it; the floor is the correlation-excess scale the audit's own exploratory contrast reached (+0.015) and declared undecidable.

The relabelling in blocks assumes dependence beyond two epochs is negligible; it does not model it. On the AR(1) control of §9 the test's size at a lag-one autocorrelation of 0.5 is measured and capped by F2 and its size at 0.7 is reported, and the lag-one autocorrelation of Q_i(k) is reported per run. A detection with *p* ≥ 0.02 whose run has a lag-one autocorrelation above 0.3 is flagged with an **autocorrelation caveat** in the result; the flag is reported, not scored. A moving-block bootstrap (block three, 2,000 draws) gives a descriptive interval for S only when a run has at least 12 eligible epochs; it undercovers and does not decide.

## 8. The falsifiers

The result is one of **qualified**, **not qualified**, **insufficient support**, **inputs unverified** or **not evaluable**, in that order of precedence.

- **F1, identity checks.** Every recorded mesh replaced by its orbit means, and a static Plummer sphere sampled at cell centres, must yield 0 eligible epochs. These cannot fail unless the implementation is defective; they are checks, not falsifiers, and are labelled so.
- **F2, false positives, on controls where relic identity carries no information by construction.** Each field control's count of detected runs must not exceed the upper end of the central 95 % binomial interval for a 5 % rate (a conservative count is acceptable; the effect-size floor makes the test conservative where the per-epoch contrast is small): independent white noise (120 runs); a static sphere seen through a Poisson deposit of 4,194,304 particles with fresh shot noise each epoch (30 runs); a shared angular drive with per-seed noise 0.7/1/1.4 (90 runs) and with per-seed drive amplitude 0.7/1/1.4 (90 runs); the same drive with a three-fold noise spread, the boundary E5b is set at (90 runs); the same drive with per-seed noise 0.5/1/2 at a level that puts the noisiest seed's own correlation near 0.07, the regime where the previous draft failed, counted over the runs E5b leaves measurable (90 runs); and the two innocent runs of a condition whose third run carries a persistent dipole (24 runs). The bound is computed for the number of runs that are measurable, since a run the gates exclude is not a test of the rule. At the level of the statistic, run-by-relic matrices whose nine entries are independent series with exchangeable columns, at mean correlation 0.3 and 0.05: independent in time (1,000 runs each, same bound) and AR(1) at lag-one autocorrelation 0.5 (1,000 runs each), where the test's size must not exceed **10 %**, twice nominal; the size at autocorrelation 0.7 is reported. The block relabelling does not remove that inflation, only halves it; the ceiling was set at twice nominal after the first draft's review measured 15 % for independent relabelling, and before the freeze. These are preconditions recorded in the synthetic receipt, whose script hash and protocol hash must equal the recorded run's. On the recorded data the stranger-as-own statistics and the strangers' sign test are **reported only**: the three column statistics of a run are computed from one matrix and are not independent tests.
- **F3, recovery.** Injection: ρ'_k on B2 = (1 − α)·ρ_k + α·P·(M_ρ/M_P), a block-mass-preserving mix with the exact passive-relic prediction, α ∈ {0, 0.002, 0.005, 0.01, 0.02, 0.05, 0.1, 0.2}. Eligibility is fixed at α = 0; the injected run's row of the matrix is recomputed, the other six entries are unchanged, and the shuffled floor is held at its baseline. **α\*** per run is the smallest α at which the full detection rule holds **and** S(α) − S(0) ≥ 0.02, so a run's baseline signal is not counted as sensitivity. The contrast can respond only to the seed-specific part of the injected template, so with template correlation ρ the recoverable increment falls roughly as (1 − ρ); the effective threshold is α\*·(1 − ρ̄), ρ̄ the run's mean absolute template correlation over eligible epochs, and an unrecovered run counts as 0.2. Over the measurable runs, at least six of them, the median effective threshold must not exceed **0.10**, and every run with ρ̄ below 0.5 must be recovered at α = 0.2. With fewer than six measurable runs F3 is **not evaluable** on the recorded data and the white-noise, deposited-sphere and shared-drive ladders are the estimator's stated sensitivity.
- **F4, robustness.** Under each variant, E1 ∈ {0.5 %, 2 %}, E2 ∈ {4, 16}, E3 ∈ {0.8, 0.95}, removal by radial classes, the hand-over scale 0.05 ∈ {0.03, 0.10}, and the E5b ratio 3 ∈ {2.5, 4}: among runs measurable under both the main and the variant configuration, a run detected with *p* < 0.02 that becomes undetected, or a run with *p* > 0.10 that becomes detected, is a violation; a variant is stable if its violations do not exceed max(1, ⌈0.05 × runs measurable under both⌉) and the F3 verdict recomputed on those same runs under main and under the variant agrees whenever both are evaluable. Measurability changes are reported, not scored. Fail if any variant is not stable. The additive-only prediction, the shell removers and no removal are reported contrasts, not variants: the plan itself calls them wrong.
- **F5, support.** A condition is measurable when all three of its runs are. If fewer than **3** conditions are measurable the result is **insufficient support**: with fewer than nine runs the F3 median is over too few numbers to mean anything. The measurable fraction is reported per condition, and the count of detected runs per condition is reported as one number.

**Qualified** requires F1 through F4 to pass with F5 satisfied and inputs verified. If any of F1–F3 cannot be evaluated the result is **not evaluable**.

## 9. Synthetic controls, run before the recorded data was scored

Needs no meshes and no GPU: `--synthetic`. (a) A Plummer sphere at cell centres as every field: 0 eligible epochs. (b) A single-cell field: fails E2. (c) Independent positive white noise, forty conditions: F2. (d) A perfect passive relic, each epoch's inner block the coarse-grained previous field plus noise: detected; this instantiates survival of the compressed configuration, the weak reading. (e) A shared angular drive, one pattern common to all seeds and epochs plus independent noise at the level that keeps the templates' correlation near 0.8: own and stranger correlations equal, not detected. (f) A persistent dipole, one fixed asymmetry per seed unchanged across epochs and no rescale-seeding, the three directions kept at least 60° apart: **detected** in every run the quality gate leaves measurable (E5b removes the conditions whose dipoles happen to align into a positive but lopsided pattern), the confound of §1. (g) Poisson-deposited static spheres: F2. (h) The shared drive with per-seed noise at two-fold, three-fold and four-fold spread, and with per-seed amplitude: F2, the controls earlier drafts failed; the four-fold case also shows how many runs E5b removes. (i) The shared drive on per-seed radial envelopes (scale 3, 4, 5 cells): fires above the null rate, the second confound of §1, a seed-specific radial structure and no seed-specific angular one. (j) One seed carrying a persistent dipole on the shared drive: that run detected, the two innocents F2. (k) The shared drive breathing in time with a seed-specific phase, and co-rotating at 5° per epoch with seed-specific offsets of 0°, 30° and 60°: named confounds of §1, reported. (l) Matrices with exchangeable columns, independent and AR(1): the size of the test. (m) Injection ladders on (c), (g) and (e): intrinsic sensitivity for uncorrelated and for correlated templates. Expected on (d) and (f): the perfect relic's unmapped contrast is far below its mapped one; the persistent dipole's unmapped contrast is comparable to or larger than its mapped one.
