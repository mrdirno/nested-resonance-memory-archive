## ABSTRACT
The Anisotropic Gyroid Prismatic Helix (AGPH) is introduced as a generative mathematical framework in which geometry, material anisotropy, and kinematic behavior emerge from a single scaling law. Traditional topology optimization relies on static cubic domains with uniform metrics. Biological systems and cosmological expansion are governed instead by variable metric fields, nested torsion, and scale-dependent bifurcations.

AGPH replaces the cubic domain with a prismatic manifold whose axial metric a(z) defines directional expansion. Inside this manifold, an anisotropically deformed gyroid field G(x) is governed by a deformation tensor A(z) and a helical rotation field θ(z). Biological joints correspond to symmetry-breaking events—kinematic phase transitions—arising from stability thresholds in the scaling field. Distal termination (digits, feet) is formalized as discrete interaction with the local tangent manifold. The model unifies limb morphology, cosmological expansion, robotic manipulation, and metamaterial design.
